## What the wave established

**The netlists are right about what connects to what.** The merge this corpus
warns about hardest did not happen — `AGND_SENSE`, `AGND_INST` and `AGND_MOD`
are never merged and `AGND_SENSE` is never declared `ref`. No netlist makes a
reference tie the corpus does not place. Every feedback topology the pages
argue about at length is correct in the netlist, including the one the pages
say three other places got wrong. Both `replicated:` numbers check out end to
end. All 113 `of:` targets resolve and no part is placed twice.

**The defects are of four kinds, and none of them is a wrong wire.**

1. **A right value on the wrong node.** The LM317 divider legs are inverted, so
   `DAC_AVDD` is 1.65 V instead of 5.21 V — below the DAC8568's absolute
   minimum. Found independently by two cold slices. Every value is correct,
   every endpoint is correct, the drawing agrees, and three checkers pass.
2. **A logical name joined to a physical one without saying so.** Four such
   joins were needed; two were marked `[derived, not cited]` and are safe
   *because* they are declared. The two nobody marked are the two that bite —
   the DAC's 0-based address map, and eleven op-amp halves that resolve to no
   pin because `section: A` is claimed by three circuits at once.
3. **A hole whose stated reason does not survive reading.** 39 unasserted
   endpoints, of which 8 are genuinely undecided; several reasons are refuted
   by a line in the same file.
4. **A check that prints a number and declines to check it.** One `section:`
   token exempts a whole row from over-use detection; a row placed zero times
   is invisible; a two-value aggregate row is unfalsifiable under a substring
   value test; a net missing a participant is invisible to both halves of the
   bidirectional check; bare-text drawing labels are never compared.

**Three slices independently arrived at `carrier/carrier`** — a circuit id
invented by one netlist, carrying 20 master assertions, with no `circuit.yaml`
and no `## Interfaces` table.

**The structural result: nothing in this repository compares the master or the
netlists to an `## Interfaces` table.** The netlists and the master were made
to agree with each other; the pages they were seeded from were not brought
along. 14 of N6's 22 findings are that one gap.
