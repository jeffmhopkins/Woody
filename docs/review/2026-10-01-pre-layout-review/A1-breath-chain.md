# A1 - The breath chain end to end: sensor, link, receive, shaper, output stage

**Slice:** A1. `U-BREATH` and its excitation (`U-REF-BREATH`, `U-BUF` A, `R-ISO-REF` network), the breath-sense link (`R1`/`R1b`, `D-TVS-BREATH`, the pair), the module receive stage (`U-DIFFRX`, `R2`–`R5`, `C_diff`/`C_cm`, `TRIM-BREATH-ZERO`), the response shaper (`U-RESP`, `R-RESP` 3.9 k, `POT-RESP` "curve") and the breath output stage (`U-BREATH-OPA`, `POT-GAIN`, `POT-OFFSET`, `R-BREATH-OFFNEG` on −12 V, `R-OUT-PROT`, `C-OUT-BREATH`), treated as one transfer function and one error budget.
**Revision measured:** 13197f8 (`git archive` copy at `scratchpad/review/A1/repo`; `tools/` pinned at the same revision).
**Tools:** `tools/check-netlist.py --strict` (0 problems; instances short: `C-DECOUPLE-CARRIER` 5/8, `U-BREATH` 1/2 = the spare); `tools/check-staleness.py` (FAIL: 2 cad only, outside this slice, 0 stale patterns); `tools/sim.py check` (PASS, 20 dirs: every results.yaml is current against its inputs); `tools/sim.py show` on the shaper. A full `tools/sim.py run` of the six breath sim dirs was **killed at its 600 s limit while still inside the shaper's 513-corner run**, so results.yaml values are the repo's own runs, hash-checked but not regenerated by me. On top of that I ran my own ngspice 42 decks, using TI's OPA2197 macromodel from the bank, in `scratchpad/review/A1/sim/`. `chain.cir` is the shaper and the output stage as netlisted, one after the other. `worst.cir` and `diode*.cir` are shaper corners. pdftotext/pdftoppm on MPXV4006DP, AN1646, INA828 and the 1N4148W sheets.

## Summary

- **The transfer function checks out at the centre detent.** Sensor 0.7665 V/kPa × in-amp effective gain 2.16106 × shaper +1 × output stage 0.503…4.02, inverted twice, gives 0 V at rest and 9.99 V at a 2.8 kPa blow with GAIN at noon and OFFSET at its true zero. My chain sim reproduces it. `inamp-full-scale`, `breath-zero-ref`, `shaper-exp-gain`, the offset table and the −12 V rail term all re-derive.
- **The shaper changes the overall gain, not just the shape of the curve.** It runs ×0.44 (log end) to ×1.64 (exp end) at a hard blow. At the commissioned gain, turning "curve" fully CW clips the jack from about 75 % of a hard blow (A1-1). At a fixed GAIN setting, the page's description of what each end does is backwards (A1-2).
- **The owner's 1.5× floor is met only by an assumption narrower than the part that is bought** (A1-3). The sim fits the diode to the 1N4448W's window. A 1N4148W at the bank's own VF limit gives 1.48× at the worst corner.
- **On the panel, OFFSET's "+" is printed at the end of travel that drives the jack to −4.91 V** (A1-4).
- **Three things are open before layout and nothing on a page tracks them:** the sensor's datasheet decoupling and output filter, which is not on the sheet (A1-5); the 2.8 kPa working point, still disputed, which every headroom claim rests on (A1-6); and the analog jack's zero drift, which has no auto-zero, no sourced bound and no pass criterion (A1-7).
- **The error budget's zero term is all sensor.** Every electronic offset drift I could bound comes to under 3 mV at the jack over 20 K. The sensor's only datasheet bound comes to about ±0.5 V (A1-7). Noise is negligible (Checked and holds).
- **Several earlier fixes reached one statement but not its neighbours:** the −12 V LED-current row (A1-8), the REF band computed at the raw gain (A1-9), ADR 0006's instrument-absent figure (A1-10), the shaper's insertion (A1-11) and the CMRR dispute (A1-12).

## Findings

### A1-1 - At the commissioned gain, POT-RESP fully CW clips the breath jack from ~75 % of a hard blow
**Severity:** high
**Node:** `BREATH_OUT` / `U-BREATH-OPA` B, with `POT-RESP` CW, `shaper-exp-gain`

Commissioning sets GAIN with `POT-RESP` at its centre click: [repo] hardware/module/breath-output-stage/breath-output-stage.md:210-213 and breath-receive-stage.md:203-213. That puts GAIN at noon (2.156×), so a hard blow reaches ~10 V: [repo] breath-output-stage.md:44-47 and :197-201. The shaper then multiplies the in-amp's output by 1.64× at a hard blow at the CW end (`shaper-exp-gain`) [sim, repo results.yaml].

- [calc] At GAIN 2.156× and CW, a hard blow asks for 4.64 × 1.64 × 2.156 = 16.4 V. The summer stops at +11.89 V: [repo] hardware/module/breath-output-stage/sim/README.md, "clip".
- [sim, scratchpad/review/A1/sim/chain.cir] I ran both stages as netlisted, with POT-GAIN at p = 0.47 and POT-OFFSET at p = 0.567 (its zero), into 100 k ∥ 330 nF.
  - Centre detent: jack 9.99 V at a hard blow (the page's number), clipping from an in-amp output of −5.48 V.
  - CW: jack 7.96 V at in-amp −2.5 V, 11.89 V at −3.5 V, clipping from −3.48 V, which is 75 % of the hard blow ≈ 2.1 kPa [calc: 3.48/(0.7665 × 2.16106)].
  - CCW: 4.36 V at a hard blow.
- The shaper page's headroom bullet says the clip is "beyond the ~2.8 kPa that real playing reaches": [repo] breath-response-shaper.md:176-180. That is true of `BREATH_SHAPED` alone. It is not true of the jack, one stage later, at the gain the commissioning procedure sets. The output-stage page's "headroom" section ([repo] breath-output-stage.md:183-193) treats a clip as the player's business with gain and offset only. The curve knob is not in that sum.

In practice, a player who commissions at the click and then turns "curve" to exp gets a flat wall over the top quarter of real playing. That is the region the exp end exists to give "more expression" in ([repo] breath-response-shaper.md:86-88).
**What would settle it:** a chain sim in the repo (shaper → output stage at noon gain), and a stated rule on the page: re-set GAIN after moving `POT-RESP`, or normalise the shaper's hard-blow gain.

### A1-2 - The shaper's "harder to get loud" (CW) and "easier to get loud" (CCW) are backwards at a fixed GAIN setting
**Severity:** medium
**Node:** `POT-RESP` / `BREATH_SHAPED`

[repo] breath-response-shaper.md:86-92: CW is "Harder to get loud; more expression at the top". CCW is "Easier to get loud; the top compresses".

The stage's ratio to linear is above 1 at every level at CW and below 1 at every level at CCW, [repo] sim/README.md table:
- CW: 1.11 at *pp*, 1.48 at *mp*, 1.64 at a hard blow.
- CCW: 0.82 at *pp*, 0.44 at a hard blow.

So at a fixed GAIN, CW is louder at every breath level and reaches the rail sooner (A1-1). CCW never gets above 44 % of the linear level at a hard blow (4.36 V at the jack [sim, chain.cir]). The descriptions hold only for the curve's *shape* after the player has re-set GAIN to put the hard blow back at the same level. No page tells the player to do that. The control is a curve plus a gain change of ×0.44…×1.64 at a hard blow.
**What would settle it:** the owner says which is intended: an un-normalised shaper with a "re-set GAIN after moving curve" rule, or a normalised one. Either way the page wording follows.

### A1-3 - The 1.5× floor holds only for a 1N4448W-grade diode at 27 °C; the bought 1N4148W at the bank's own VF limit gives 1.48×
**Severity:** medium
**Node:** `D-RESP` (1N4148W-E3-08), `R-RESP`, `shaper-exp-gain`

- [repo] hardware/module/breath-response-shaper/sim/sims.yaml:23,29: `is_d` is fitted to "the 1N4448W's guaranteed window, 0.62–0.72 V at 5 mA" and varied only across it (0.94–8.1 nA).
- [ds 1N4148W-DIOTEC.pdf p.2]: that window is the **1N4448W** column. The 1N4148W column gives only "< 0.715 V" at 1 mA, with no minimum.
- [repo] D-RESP row: the bought part is Vishay's 1N4148W-E3-08, whose sheet gives only VF ≤ 1 V at 10 mA.
- [calc] With N = 1.8, the sim's slowest diode (0.72 V at 5 mA) is 0.645 V at 1 mA. A 1N4148W at its 0.715 V limit is IS = 1e-3/exp(0.715/(1.8 × 0.02585)) = 2.1e-10 A.
- [sim, scratchpad/review/A1/sim/worst.cir] At the repo's worst resistor/pot corner, the sim's slowest diode reproduces its 1.5066× exactly. The 0.715 V diode gives **1.477×**. At the other divider corner it gives 1.495×. At nominal resistors it gives 1.588×, against 1.618×.
- [sim, diode_cold.cir] Running at 15 °C instead of 27 °C costs a further ~0.005–0.01 (the knee moves up). sims.yaml has no temperature corner.
- [repo] sim/README.md: 4.3 k already fails at 1.49×. So the margin at 3.9 k (1.5066 against 1.5) is smaller than either the part's unspecified VF spread or a 12 K temperature step.

**What would settle it:** buy a part whose VF is specified near 1 mA (e.g. a true 1N4448W), or extend `is_d` to the 1N4148W's limit and add a temperature corner, then re-pick `R-RESP` (3.6 k is the next E24 down).

### A1-4 - The panel's OFFSET "+" mark sits at the end of travel that drives the breath jack negative
**Severity:** medium
**Node:** `POT-OFFSET` (CW pin = `DAC_AVDD`), panel marks `scale OFFSET+` / `scale OFFSET-`

- [repo] hardware/module/breath-output-stage/netlist.yaml: `DAC_AVDD: POT-OFFSET.CW` and `AGND_MOD: POT-OFFSET.CCW`. The page's own table, [repo] breath-output-stage.md:126-130, gives Full CCW **+5.06 V** and Full CW **−4.91 V**. The output-stage sim reproduces both to 1 mV ([repo] sim/README.md).
- [repo] mechanical/module/export/panel-art.echo:68-69: "mark OFFSET-" at x = 19.91 and "mark OFFSET+" at x = 30.59. Each sits at an end stop 140° either side of the top ([repo] config/module.yaml `art.mark.angle`). So "−" is at the CCW (left) end and "+" at the CW (right) end, the attenuverter convention ADR 0026 point 5 names.
- Both cannot be right. As netlisted, turning OFFSET towards "+" moves the jack negative. By contrast, `POT-GAIN`'s CW pin is `BREATH_SHAPED` (more gain clockwise), which follows the usual convention.
- If the fix is to swap `POT-OFFSET`'s ends on the sheet, the true zero moves to ~19° *counter*-clockwise of centre. Any legend placement that relies on "past centre" moves with it ([repo] breath-output-stage.md:131,137-140; panel.md:137).

**What would settle it:** check the sheet's CW/CCW pin names against the R0904N's pin-1/pin-3 rotation sense on the footprint. Then swap the pot's ends (or the marks) and re-run the offset sim.

### A1-6 - `breath-working-point` is DISPUTED, but the shaper, output stage and their sims treat 2.8 kPa as fact and cite it to a document that does not contain it
**Severity:** medium
**Node:** `breath-working-point`; `shaper-exp-gain`; `POT-GAIN` working point

- [repo] config/figures.yaml:212-219: `status: disputed`. The candidates include "2.8 kPa (cited to ADR 0003, which does not contain it ...)", "0-5 kPa" and "3-4 kPa", decided by E2.
- [repo] breath-output-stage.md:44 says "Real playing only reaches about 2.8 kPa ... (ADR 0003)". [repo] ADR 0003:144-157 says "Normal wind-controller playing sits around 0–5 kPa" and that the hard blow "is `breath-working-point`, open until E2". So the citation is the one the register already flags.
- The same value appears without the dispute in [repo] breath-receive-stage.md:206, breath-response-shaper.md:180, and response-shaper sims.yaml:95 (`v_hard: 4.64`, "beyond real playing (~2.8 kPa)"). Only breath-adc.md:41 carries the "[one candidate ... open until E2]" tag.
- What moves if a candidate other than 2.8 kPa wins [calc, slope 0.7665 × 2.16106 = 1.6565 V/kPa at the in-amp]:
  - The shaper's worst-corner clip at −6.51 V is **3.93 kPa**, inside the "3-4 kPa" candidate and the "0-5 kPa" range.
  - The output stage at noon gain clips at in-amp −5.48 V [sim, chain.cir] = **3.31 kPa**.
  - The owner's "1.5× at a hard blow" is evaluated at a pressure nobody has measured. The ratio rises with pressure until the clip, so a higher working point helps the floor and hurts the headroom.

**What would settle it:** E2's manometer test ([repo] ADR 0003:147-157). Until then, the pages should cite the figure as disputed, as breath-adc.md does.

### A1-7 - The analog jack's zero has no auto-zero, an unsourced drift figure, and a datasheet bound ~25× larger than the figure; it dominates the error budget
**Severity:** medium
**Node:** `TRIM-BREATH-ZERO` / `BREATH_OUT` at rest

- [repo] breath-receive-stage.md:215-218: "on the order of 20 mV in 10 V over a full warm-up ... **unverified** ... ~0.5 mV/K". The U-BREATH BOM row: "TcOffset IS UNSOURCED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS ... NEEDS A BENCH MEASUREMENT".
- [ds MPXV4006DP.pdf p.4, Table 1 and Note 5]: accuracy ±2.46 %VFSS *with* auto-zero, only for "a maximum temperature change of ±5°C between autozero and measurement". Without auto-zero it is ±5.0 %VFSS.
- [calc] Sensor-to-jack gain at the commissioned setting is 2.16106 × 2.156 = 4.66. The two datasheet bounds are ±0.0246 × 4.6 V = ±113 mV at the sensor, **±0.53 V at the jack**, and ±230 mV, **±1.07 V at the jack**. These are total-accuracy bounds, not a drift spec, but they are the only bound in the bank. The body warms 10–20 K ([repo] ADR 0003:219), beyond the ±5 °C the 2.46 % assumes.
- The analog path is trimmed once "at build" ([repo] breath-receive-stage.md:203-204). Only the digital copy auto-zeros (ADR 0003:85-96; ADR 0006:294-317). ROADMAP's "Stale breath zero" mitigation ([repo] ROADMAP.md:223) names that firmware auto-zero, which does not reach the jack.
- A positive drift leaves a VCA open at rest. That is the failure the REF-trim section was written to remove ([repo] breath-receive-stage.md:151-157).
- E2's warm-up row ([repo] ROADMAP.md:196) only tells "falls" from "drifts". It sets no pass threshold for the jack.
- For comparison, every electronic term I could bound comes to under 3 mV at the jack over 20 K (Checked and holds), so the zero budget is entirely the sensor's.

**What would settle it:** add a jack-at-rest log across E2's 20-minute warm-up, with a pass threshold. The page carries the result, or the datasheet bound until then.

## Checked and holds

- **Sensor transfer function.** [ds MPXV4006DP p.4] Voff 0.152/0.265/0.378 V, VFSS 4.6 V typ, 766 mV/kPa. [calc] 5 × (0.1533 × 6 + 0.053) = 4.864 V and 0.7665 V/kPa match `sensor-full-scale` and `breath-sensor-slope`. Hard blow at 2.8 kPa: 0.265 + 2.146 = 2.411 V.
- **In-amp gain.** [calc] 1 + 50k/42.2k = 2.18483; × 1M/1.011M = 2.16106; × 4.599 = 9.939 V, matching `inamp-full-scale`. 0.265 × 2.16106 = 0.5727 V, matching `breath-zero-ref`. 2.146 × 2.16106 = 4.638 V at a hard blow.
- **INA828 headroom.** Output swing (V−)+0.15 [ds INA828 p.6] against −9.94 V on ±12 V, fine. Input range (V−)+2…(V+)−2 [ds p.5] against 0–5 V inputs, fine. R-RESP-IN ∥ divider load 10 k = the datasheet's RL. REF driven by a follower, [ds] "< 5 Ω".
- **REF trim range.** [calc] 5.21 × 10/52.2 = 0.998 V, and 0.915 V with the track 10 % low, covers 0.817 V. 25-turn ≈ 40 mV/turn.
- **Shaper.** At centre the stage is unity: (−10k/20k)(−20k/10k). [sim, chain.cir] CW 1.641× at a hard blow (repo 1.6409); worst corner 1.5066× reproduced exactly (worst.cir). [calc, hand KCL with 5 k divider source and the pot load] ≈1.60× at Vd 0.6 V, consistent. Clip at in-amp −6.95/−6.51 V ≥ 1.3 × 4.64 = 6.03 V. Centre null 5 % of rotation, × 280° = 14°, matching the page. The netlist's diode orientation matches the deck.
- **Output stage.** [calc] Offset: +5.062 / +0.606 / −4.912 V, zero at p = 0.567. Gain 0.503…4.02. Noon p = 0.47 → 2.156×. [sim, chain.cir] the hard blow gives 9.99 V at the detent. The −12 V term is 4.09 mV for 9.7 mV ([repo] ADR 0027:158). C-OUT-BREATH: 1/(2π × 1k × 330n) = 482 Hz, cap on the jack side of R-OUT-PROT (netlist).
