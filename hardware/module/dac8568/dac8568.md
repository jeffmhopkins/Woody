# DAC8568C — schematic

## Interfaces

Every net that crosses this circuit's boundary. Quantities appear **only** as a
citation into `config/figures.yaml` — this table names nodes, it does not
restate values.

The `Dir` and `Peer` columns are defined once in
[`hardware/README.md`](../../README.md#the-interfaces-table).

| Node | Dir | Peer | Figure | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SCLK_DAC`, `DIN`, `SYNC` | in | `module/digital-and-supervision`, `interfaces/spi-link` | — | Buffered SPI, out of the 74AHCT125. The DAC-side three of the six `R-SPI-PULL` hold these when the buffer is Hi-Z. **`SCLK_DAC` is not `SCLK`**, the umbilical conductor on the buffer's input side |
| `DAC AVDD` | in | `module/power-entry` | `dac-rail` | The LM317 rail, at the `AVDD` pin. The figure's `floor` is a C-grade condition, not a preference |
| `CLR` | in | `module/link-supervision` | — | Active low, held **inactive** by `R-CLR-PU`. Nothing drives it — the part that did is not fitted. `LK-CLR` asserts it by hand |
| `LDAC` | ref | — | — | `R-LDAC`, a 0 Ω strap to `GND`. Not a pull-up; see [`notes.md`](notes.md) |
| `VREFOUT` | out | `module/pitch-stage` | — | The internal reference, and the node the DAC's own full scale is referenced to |
| `DAC ch1` | out | `module/pitch-stage` | — | Into `R-OPAMP-IN` |
| `DAC ch2`–`ch5` | out | `module/mod-channels` | — | One signal channel per mod channel, into `R-OPAMP-IN` |
| `DAC ch7` | out | `module/mod-channels` | `mod-reference` | The mod channels' shared offset reference |
| `AGND_MOD` | ref | `module/power-entry` | `dig-gnd-topology` | The analog star, single tie |

## The circuit

*Connectivity is **[`netlist.yaml`](netlist.yaml)**, not this drawing.
The drawing is a representation of it, `tools/check-netlist.py` checks that
the two agree, and where they do not the netlist wins.*

*The DAC half of the drawing on
[`../digital-and-supervision/digital-and-supervision.md`](../digital-and-supervision/digital-and-supervision.md),
moved verbatim 2026-09-21. The umbilical, the buffer and the `DIG_GND` run
either side of it stayed there; the `│` column at the left is that run.*
