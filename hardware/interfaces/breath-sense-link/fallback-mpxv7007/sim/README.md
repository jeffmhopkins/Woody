# MPXV7007DP fallback — simulation

**Not the live design.** This simulates `U-BREATH`'s documented fallback
(issue #30): an MPXV7007DP in the same footprint, `U-BUF` B made into an
offset-and-gain stage, and `R-ZERO-TOP` reduced.
[`breath-sense-link.md`](../../breath-sense-link.md), *Fallback: MPXV7007DP*,
derives it and holds its results.

`sims.yaml` says what is simulated and what every run must show, and
`fallback.cir` is the deck. `results.yaml` is generated by
`python3 tools/sim.py run hardware/interfaces/breath-sense-link/fallback-mpxv7007/sim`.
`docs/reference/tooling.md` §5 explains the tool.

## Where the values come from

**Every part the live design fits is read from its netlist:**

- `R1`, `R1b` and `C-SENSOR-OUT` from this circuit's;
- the ADC divider and `C-AA-ADC` from `carrier/breath-adc`'s;
- the receive stage from `module/breath-receive-stage`'s.

**The fallback's four values are params**, because no sheet carries them:
`R-BUFB-FB`, `R-BUFB-VS`, `R-BUFB-GND`, and `R-ZERO-TOP` as it would become.
Applying the fallback puts them on the sheets. This deck should then read them
from the netlists like every other sim.

**The models:**

- **The sensor** is its banked transfer function, behavioural
  (`datasheets/analog/MPXV7007DP.pdf` p.5). Its offset is varied by ±0.17 V,
  the `V_off` band from p.2.
- **The op-amps and the in-amp** are TI's OPA2197 and INA828 macromodels.

## Why transients

TI's INA828 model finds no operating point for this chain at rest. Gmin and
source stepping both fail, and ngspice's fallback then reports a false
operating point: the ADC node came out at 17 mV under a 0.66 V source, through
a resistive divider. So every reading is taken from a transient started from
zero, with the rails ramped over 1 ms, after the chain has settled
(`fallback.cir`).

## What it shows

| Sim | What | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| `fallback` | the stage, the link and the in-amp, with commissioning (`REF` set to null the rest output), then 2.8 and 6 kPa; `VS` raised 5 %; a 2.8 kPa step | (1) the slope is within 0.5 % of `breath-sensor-slope`; (2) the in-amp's 6 kPa output is within 1 % of `inamp-full-scale`; (3) the lowest pedestal stays above the OPA2197's 125 mV swing; (4) the fallback trimmer reaches the highest null; (5) it is ratiometric; (6) the step overshoot is under 5 % and it settles in under 100 µs. It also records that a part at the top of its offset band clips the ADC copy just below 6 kPa |
| `drop-in` | the MPXV7007DP with nothing else changed | it records why this fails: the live trimmer cannot null it, and the slope is 37 % of the fitted part's |

## What a result is worth

This is a screen, not a bench result. The sensor here is its datasheet
transfer function: it has no noise, no 1 ms response and no temperature
behaviour. The ADC's sampling is `breath-adc/sim`'s and is not repeated here.
