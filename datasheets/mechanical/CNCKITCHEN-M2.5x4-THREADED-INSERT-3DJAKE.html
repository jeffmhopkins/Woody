Description

The CNC-milled threaded inserts made of high-quality brass alloy are practical accessories that can be used to convert 3D-printed plastic parts (made of PLA, PETG, ABS, etc.) into practical and versatile components.

The cadmium and lead-free threaded inserts are inserted or melted into place with a soldering iron. They increase the strength of the resulting connection by connecting the threaded insert to the material and staying firmly in place.

Tip: For maximum connection strength, install the brass inserts at a temperature that is 10-20°C higher than the printing temperature. CNC Kitchen also recommends that only 90% of the threaded inserts should be melted with a soldering iron - the rest can be fitted into the component with a tool.

Advantages at a glance:

  • suitable for all thermoplastics
  • also suitable for SLA resin (Note: the inserts are glued in this case!)
  • cadmium-free and lead-free
  • REACH and RoHS compliant
  • durable and robust
  • creates strong connections

► The set contains 100 pieces of M2.5 standard (M2.5x4.0) threaded inserts.
